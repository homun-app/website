title: Welcome to Homun
description: Homun is a team of AI bots that works on your computer. Each bot has one job, they share a contained computer and a memory, and messages leave only as drafts you confirm.
---

Homun is **your bot team**. You give each bot one job — sort the morning mail, prepare
tomorrow's meetings, watch for mentions of your project — and it does that job on your
computer. Each bot has:

- **a computer to work in**: one contained box, shared by every bot, with a browser, a
  terminal and files, so bots can do real work without touching your system;
- **access to the apps you connect**, such as Gmail, Trello, Slack or your own MCP
  servers;
- **a memory** that keeps what it learns with the source and the date;
- **approvals**: you decide how much a bot may do on its own, and messages to other
  people wait as drafts.

You choose the model behind every bot: a cloud provider, a model on your network, or the
model built into Homun, which runs entirely on your machine.

:::note[Coming from Homun 2?]
Homun 0.3 is a complete renewal. Conversations and bots from Homun 2 are **not
imported**: they stay in the Homun 2 folder on your Mac, and Homun does not touch them.
Channels (WhatsApp, Telegram) and the self-hosted server of Homun 2 are not part of
0.3.
:::
