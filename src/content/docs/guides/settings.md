Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Each section covers one area. Apps, MCP servers and
skills live in the **Plugin** page of the sidebar, not here.

## General

- **Appearance**: theme (System, Light, Dark) and language (System, Italiano, English).
- **At startup**: **Start at login** keeps your bots active even when the window is
  closed — macOS may ask you to enable it in Login Items. **Version and updates** has
  **Check for updates** and **Get pre-release versions**.
- **Initial guide**: resume the steps you skipped, or start the guide over. Starting over
  does not touch your bots or your data.
- **Delivery**: your time zone and a delivery window. Non-urgent messages from routines
  wait for that window.
- **Notifications**: choose, bot by bot, whether to hear from it when it finishes or
  waits for you.
- **Advanced**: steps in chat, the look-around on return, routine pause after days away,
  and web search.
- **Bot computer**: where commands run, and which engine runs the box. See
  [The bots' computer](/guides/local-computer/).
- **Disk space**: how much the bots' computer, the local model and the memory model take,
  and how to free space.

## Models

The model your bots use, its backups, the model that reviews approvals in Automatic
mode, the providers you connected and the built-in model. See [Models](/guides/models/).

## Approvals

When a bot should ask you: **Ask**, **Automatic** or **Never ask**, your own rules, and
the consents you saved with **Always**. See [Approvals & safety](/guides/security/).

## Vault

The logins, secrets and contacts your bots can use. Bots see only the names of secrets
and logins, never the values. See [Approvals & safety](/guides/security/#the-vault).

## Routines

The main bot's routines: schedule, next and last run, on and off. See
[Routines](/guides/automations/).

## Usage & costs

What models cost: calls, tokens and cost by model, by bot or by source.
