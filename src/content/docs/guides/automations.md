---
title: Routines
description: Recurring work on a schedule, suggested by Homun or created by you.
---

A **routine** runs a request on a schedule: every weekday at 8:00, every Monday morning,
the first day of the month. The bot does the work as if you had just asked, and the chat
shows a small line such as *Routine "Morning inbox"*.

## Creating a routine

- **Ask for it**: *"Every weekday at 8, sort my inbox."* The bot shows one card with the
  name, the schedule and the request. Allow it and the routine is born active; when it is
  born paused instead, the chat shows a line with **Activate**.
- **From the bot panel**: press **New routine**.
- **From a suggestion**: Homun notices what you ask again and again and suggests
  **Create a routine**: the message is ready to send, and the routine is born paused.

## Managing routines

The bot panel lists that bot's routines with the next run, and lets you pause and resume
them. **Settings › Routines** lists the main bot's routines: schedule, next and last run,
on and off.

## When results arrive

Non-urgent messages from routines wait for your **delivery window**, which you set in
**Settings › General › Delivery** together with your time zone.

## When you are away

After some hours away, the bot takes a look at what changed and tells you, in a line
marked *When you came back*. After some days away, routines pause by themselves, and the
bot offers to resume them when you return. Both timers live in **Settings › General ›
Advanced**.
