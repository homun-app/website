A **team** groups bots that work on the same thing. Each bot keeps its own job, memory
and computer; the team gives them a shared chat and a **coordinator**.

## Creating a team

Teams are born in chat. Ask your bot for one, for example *"Make a team to organise the
offsite: one bot for the venue, one for travel."* The bot sets up the team, with its
members and a coordinator, and the team appears in the sidebar under **Teams**.

## How a team works

- You write to the team. The **coordinator** answers and splits the work among the
  members.
- To speak to a specific member, write **@** followed by its name.
- The members talk to each other in the team chat. You receive a **summary for you**
  instead of every message.

Each member still asks for approval before anything goes out, exactly as it would on its
own. Open the members panel to see who is in the team and what each one is doing.
