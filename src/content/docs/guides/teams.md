A **team** groups bots that work on the same thing. Each bot keeps its own job and its
own memory; the team gives them a shared chat and a **coordinator**, and they all work in
the same [bots' computer](/guides/local-computer/).

## Creating a team

Teams are born in chat. Ask your bot for one, for example *"Make a team to organise the
offsite: one bot for the venue, one for travel."* The bot sets up the team, with its
members and a coordinator, and the team appears in the sidebar under **Team**.

## How a team works

- You write to the team: the **coordinator** answers, and your message reaches the
  members too. To speak to one member only, write **@** followed by its name.
- The members talk to each other in the team chat. When the work is done, the
  coordinator sends you one **summary for you**.

A team has its own autonomy setting — the default, **Free inside the computer**, lets the
members work freely inside the bots' computer. Some things always come to you: forms with
your data, logins and the Vault, payments, messages and posts going out, actions on your
Mac outside the computer, deleting a bot. Open the members panel to see who is in the
team and what each one is doing.
