Engramma 0.6.19-beta.1 — the capture behind the Engramma page
============================================================

The terminal lines, the log rows and the figures of /engramma/ come from
this session: the released binary (commit 5d0e02a), installed by the release
installer into a temporary folder, on a fresh data root (ENGRAMMA_ROOT and
ENGRAMMA_MODEL_DIR inside <tmp>), with the synthetic notes of the page
(Gaia, Milano, Torino, Acme). The CLI prints in English since 0.6.19:
commands and output are verbatim, and every command line is a shell line
(queries and titles are quoted). What was changed: paths are shortened
(<tmp> is the temporary folder of the session, <node> the node executable),
the answer of an MCP call is printed on one line, and the capture script's
own bookkeeping is left out.

The notes are the synthetic notes of the page, in Italian: they are the
user's data, and only what the CLI prints is English. Four bodies — Ufficio
a Torino, Trasloco: scatoloni da Torino, Nuovo ufficio, CFO di Acme — were
written again for this capture: the files of the 0.6.17 session went with
its temporary folder, and that capture never printed them. Titles, tags,
validity, sources and the [[names]] they mention are the ones that capture
showed; the figures that depend on those four bodies are new. The embedding
model (granite-embedding-97m-multilingual-r2, Apache-2.0) was pulled into
<tmp>/models before S0; S16 shows the pull.

The clients of the session
--------------------------
Story 1 is real on the proposing side: Claude Code 2.1.332 proposed through
its own MCP client (`--mcp-config`, headless), and the MCP Inspector 2.16.0
read the same cell. Codex is not installed on the machine. The other actors
— Cursor, OpenCode, ChatGPT, Homun, and Codex as the reader of S16 — were a
stdio MCP driver carrying the configuration each client would carry.

The engine signs the actor of the configuration, never what the client says
it is, so the engine side of every scene is the real one; the client side of
the proposals by Cursor and ChatGPT, and of the reads by Codex, OpenCode and
Homun, is not (`declared client: site-capture-driver 0.0.1`). The reviewer
`local` is a fixture that answers with a fixed rule (accept what the user
stated with confidence 0.9; under the policy's minimum it stays in the
queue; the rest goes to the person), not a model.

What the session showed, beyond the page
----------------------------------------
- `revert --by mcp:<actor>` is a usage error (exit 2): decisions are signed
  by who decides (user@cli, reviewer:<name>), never by who proposed. The
  message names the way: `--proposed-by`, which undoes the decisions taken
  on that actor's proposals (S10).
- A read leaves no trace in the log: `verify` counts the same operations
  before and after the search of S2, by CLI, and around the read of S6, by
  MCP.
- `revert <id> --dry-run` writes nothing: `verify` counts the same
  operations before and after the preview (S14).
- Two corrections of the same note: the first is accepted; the second is
  refused (exit 4: `… is not the current version — already corrected by …`)
  and stays in the queue, and `conflicts` reports no copy. Revert the first
  and the second is decidable again; reject the other and the tombstone
  keeps its text (`rejected (tombstone)`). Conflict copies come from two
  devices (S11, S11b, S14, S14b).
- A proposal that is accepted has its vector at once (`embedded: 1 (pending:
  0)`), so a question with no word in common finds it without `engramma
  embed`; `embed` only takes what still waits (S6, S12).
