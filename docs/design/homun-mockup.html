A team, not a chatbot

Your bots work together. You approve what matters.

A coordinator hands out the work. Each bot does its part with your apps, its own computer and one shared memory. What changes the world waits for your OK, and every step is in the activity log.

    Questions

    Asked before you download.

    Can a bot act without asking?

    Not by default. Before an action that changes something, a bot shows an approval card with what it wants to do and why: allow it once, for this turn, always, or deny it. Messages to other people become drafts that leave only when you press Send.

    Does it need an API key?

    Not with a local model: the built-in one, Ollama or LM Studio. For a cloud model you use your own key.

    Where does my data stay?

    On your computer. It goes out only to the apps you connect and to the cloud model you pick, if you pick one.

    Which systems does it run on?

    macOS today; Windows and Linux in beta.
